About the Academy

A practical learning space for people who want to understand software quality and grow their QA careers.

Our purpose

Software Testing 4 U is an online learning initiative designed to make software testing knowledge accessible, structured and practical. We aim to support freshers entering QA as well as experienced testers strengthening their technical skills.

Meet your trainer

Kumar Gaurav is a software testing professional with 7+ years of experience. His professional experience includes manual testing, web and mobile application testing, API testing and test automation.

Teaching philosophy

Understand the concept, see it demonstrated, practise independently, apply it to a project and explain it confidently.

What learners can expect

  • Clear explanations of testing concepts and tools.
  • Hands-on examples and practical assignments.
  • Industry-inspired project scenarios.
  • Guidance on technical interviews and learning portfolios.
